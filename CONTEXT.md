# Rime & Run
An alpine arcade expedition through Juniper Basin.
## Language
**Run**: One descent from the ridge to the refuge. _Avoid_: round.
**Gate**: A pair of pennants defining a passage on the slalom course.
**Landing**: The return to snow after a hop or ramp launch.
**Combo**: Consecutive successful gates or trick landings, broken by a miss or crash.
**Rimehorn**: A playful mountain creature that chases skiers toward the refuge.
**Refuge**: The safe finish of a descent.
